# Company context

A one-person AI automation consultancy based in India, working with clients in the UK, Europe and Australia. The founder is an engineer who builds agent systems, n8n workflows, CRM enrichment and document-processing pipelines for small companies (5 to 50 people). Revenue comes mostly through Upwork contracts and referrals; one retained client on a CRM and finance-automation engagement, a few fixed-price projects a quarter.

Tools in use: Claude Code and the Anthropic API for building, n8n for client workflows, Apify for scraping and enrichment, Attio and HubSpot on the client side, Gmail and Google Sheets, GitHub, Vercel for small apps. No CRM of its own: leads live in Upwork messages, LinkedIn DMs, email threads and a spreadsheet.

Capacity: one person, roughly 25 billable hours a week, the rest on proposals, follow-ups and open source. No sales or marketing staff. Publishes on LinkedIn irregularly. Has public open source repos that bring occasional inbound.

Constraints: cannot spend more than a few hours a week on sales. Will not buy expensive sales tooling. Everything that contacts a lead must be reviewed by the founder before it goes out. Data about prospects must stay in tools the founder already uses.
