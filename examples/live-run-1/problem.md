Inbound leads from Upwork invitations, LinkedIn messages and referrals are not followed up consistently. Some get a reply within an hour, some wait days, some are forgotten. Proposals take two to four hours each to research and write, so only a few get sent, and there is no record of which ones converted or why. Design the smallest system that captures every lead in one place, drafts the first reply and the proposal for the founder to approve, follows up on a schedule, and shows what is working, using the tools already in use and no more than two hours of founder time a week.
